# Create the New School Pilot Pitch Deck

## Deliverables
- Create an editable PowerPoint deck and a matching PDF for Evaan to send directly to a school administrator.
- Keep the language reusable for any school and concise enough for a short introductory conversation.

## Story and content
1. **Open with the purpose:** recognize essential school staff whose work is often unseen.
2. **Show the experience:** use real screenshots from the live Now We See You home, gallery, profile, appreciation, and nomination pages.
3. **Make the model feel achievable:** present a two-person pilot, one artist and one journalist, supported by one school administrator. A club is not required.
4. **Explain the workflow:** select one staff member, secure consent, interview and create the portrait, review, publish, and share through the existing platform and QR system.
5. **Clarify the administrator’s role:** approve the pilot, identify or hire the two contributors, introduce the first participant, and retain final publication approval.
6. **End with a small ask:** begin with one person and one story, then decide whether to continue.

## Visual direction
- Match the existing warm cream, charcoal, and lilac identity with editorial portraiture and restrained typography.
- Use screenshots as evidence, not decoration; crop them to emphasize the exact page feature being discussed.
- Build approximately 7 slides with minimal copy, varied layouts, and a clear “why first, how second” progression.

## Technical details
- Generate the `.pptx` as a fully editable 16:9 presentation and export the same deck as PDF.
- Embed all screenshots and images so both files work offline.
- Validate the PowerPoint structure, extract its text to check content order, render every slide, inspect every slide visually, fix identified issues, and re-render before delivery.
- Do not modify the website, frontend, backend, or database.
